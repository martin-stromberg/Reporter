Description texts

Short text

Reporter is a local RSS/Atom feed reader built as a .NET MAUI app for iOS (iPhone only for now): subscribe to feeds, read articles, save posts for later — without its own backend, without a user account, without tracking and without advertising.

Long text

Reporter offers one central place to subscribe to RSS and Atom feeds, read articles and save posts for later. The app follows the system appearance (light/dark) and supports German and English — either matching the system language or via a manual language selection in the settings. Article text that has already been synced stays readable even without an internet connection.

Five tabs structure the app: “Unread” shows new articles with a category filter, “Feeds” manages subscriptions including their health status, “Later” collects saved posts, “Categories” organizes the feeds, and “Settings” bundles retention period, keyword filters, background refresh, notifications with quiet hours, color scheme and language.

Reporter works without its own backend and without a user account: all data lives in local SQLite databases on the device; on iOS the user data is included in the iCloud backup. The app collects no telemetry, contains no tracking, no analytics SDKs and no advertising.
